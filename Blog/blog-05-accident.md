# What to Do After an Accident on a Rented Bike

*Category: Deposit & Safety (cluster) — slug: deposit-safety*

An accident is the last thing you want to think about before a trip, but knowing the right order of steps in advance means you'll actually follow them if you ever need to — instead of panicking. Here's the sequence that matters.

## Step 1: Check for Injuries First

Before anything involving the bike, make sure you and anyone else involved are okay. If anyone is injured, get medical help before worrying about the vehicle.

## Step 2: Move to Safety

If the bike is rideable and it's safe to do so, get it and yourself off the road and out of traffic before dealing with anything else.

## Step 3: Contact Us Immediately

Message us on WhatsApp or Telegram as soon as you're safe — this is the same channel our team already uses to coordinate day to day, so you'll get a real, fast response rather than an automated dead end. Tell us where you are and what happened, and we'll talk you through the next steps for your specific situation.

## Step 4: Document the Scene

If you're able to safely do so, photos of the bike, the surrounding area, and (if another party was involved) their vehicle and details go a long way — both for us and, if needed, for any official report.

## Step 5: Involve the Police If Required

For any accident involving another party, injury, or significant damage, a police report is often necessary — we can advise you on this based on your specific situation when you message us.

## A Bit of Perspective

Bali's traffic can look chaotic if you're not used to it, but the actual pace is slow, and the driving culture — among scooters and cars alike — tends to be patient and forgiving rather than aggressive. From what we see day to day, serious accidents are relatively uncommon. That's not a reason to let your guard down, but it's also not something to spend your whole trip worrying about — ride at a sensible speed, wear your helmet, and stick to the basics, and the odds are firmly in your favor.

## What Happens Next

What you actually owe, and whether insurance applies, depends on the specifics of the accident and your rental — this isn't something we can answer with a single flat rule for every situation, which is exactly why step 3 (contacting us right away) matters so much. We'll walk you through your specific case honestly and directly.
